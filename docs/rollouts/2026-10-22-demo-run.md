## What was different from what we expected

We expected all three to escalate, because we assumed the demo vendor was not approved on-chain. DEMO-1-A was paid instead. The agent checks the vendor on the contract before paying, so that check passed: the owner's wallet was already an approved vendor of the original contract. Step 3 of "Try it in 5 minutes" said the vendor is not yet approved and nothing is paid by itself. For a new owner that is the normal case; for this one it was not. The page now says both outcomes.

## What else the run showed

- The first read of `GET /businesses/ceedebooks/traction` after the run listed DEMO-1-A in its live figures and "latest paid". That endpoint counted every origin, so demo and manual invoices (including three older test runs relabelled `demo`) were being added to the live figures. Traction is the agent's own traffic only (rule 14). It was fixed in v1.3.1.2: `live` and `latest_paid` count origin `agent` only, and manual and demo are shown apart in `not_traction`. The two `update-traction` posts in the Arc Canteen CLI history predate that endpoint, so none used the wrong numbers.
- `GET /stats` reported 6 demo invoices processed: these three plus three older test invoices relabelled `demo` (INV-TEST-6, 7 and 8).
- The public agent totals (`/stats`) did not move, as designed.
